FICTIONAL TEST POLICY — FOR DEVELOPMENT ONLY

Change-of-mind returns:
Sale-priced items cannot be returned solely because the customer changed their mind.

Damaged items:
Customers must report damage within 14 calendar days after delivery. Sale-priced items are eligible under this damaged-item policy.

Required evidence:
Customers must provide the order number and photographs showing the damage.

Resolution:
After reviewing the submitted evidence and confirming that the item arrived damaged, the retailer will offer a replacement or refund.

Reconsideration:
Customers may reply to the denial message to request reconsideration. This policy does not specify a separate reconsideration deadline.
